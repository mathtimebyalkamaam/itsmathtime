About Alka Sharma

Hello! I'm Alka Sharma, your dedicated mathematics educator and the face behind MathTime by Alka Ma'am. With an M.Sc. in Mathematics, B.Ed., and CTET certification, I bring 15 years of proven experience to the classroom. My passion is to demystify complex topics and build a strong foundation for lifelong learning, believing that every student can excel in mathematics.

As a results-oriented professional, my teaching style focuses on clarity, conceptual understanding, and practical application. I am known for my effective communication, fostering a positive learning environment, and consistently maintaining excellent relations with students, parents, faculty, and administrators. I break down difficult problems into easy-to-understand steps, using real-world examples to make learning relatable and enjoyable.

Committed to continuous development and adapting to evolving educational needs, I've attended over 15 professional workshops across India. Whether you're preparing for board exams, competitive tests, or just want to improve your math skills, you'll find a supportive and engaging environment here. Join our growing community on YouTube and let's make math your favorite subject!
