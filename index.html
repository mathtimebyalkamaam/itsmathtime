Got Questions? We've Got Clear Answers

Everything students and parents need to know about MathTime, CBSE curriculum coverage, and learning with Alka Ma'am.

Who is Alka Sharma and what is MathTime?

Alka Sharma (M.Sc. in Mathematics, B.Ed., CTET Certified) is a senior mathematics educator with over 15 years of classroom teaching and board exam mentoring experience. MathTime is her educational platform and YouTube channel dedicated to making CBSE Class 7, 8, 9, and 10 mathematics intuitive, high-scoring, and stress-free for school students.

Which classes and syllabus are covered on MathTime?

MathTime covers the complete CBSE (NCERT) curriculum for Classes 7, 8, 9, and 10. Core topics include Real Numbers, Polynomials, Linear & Quadratic Equations, Triangles, Circles, Coordinate Geometry, Trigonometry, Surface Areas & Volumes, Statistics, and Probability.

Are the video tutorials and NCERT solutions completely free?

Yes! All 200+ video lessons, concept walkthroughs, and shorts on the official YouTube channel (@its.mathtime) are 100% free and accessible to students, parents, and teachers everywhere.

How does Alka Ma'am help students prepare for CBSE Class 10 Board Exams?

Alka Ma'am focuses on NCERT step-marking rubrics, common board exam pitfalls, previous year question (PYQ) patterns, geometry proof structures, and time allocation tactics across Sections A through E to help students aim for 95%+ marks. Explore our Class 10 Board Prep Hub.

Does MathTime teach mental math and speed calculation tricks?

Yes! MathTime features dedicated tutorials on speed math hacks, rapid multiplication, squaring numbers in seconds, short division for HCF/LCM, and estimation techniques. Check out our Math Tricks Page.

How can students ask doubts or request specific math topics?

You can send a message directly using the Contact Form below or email info.onesharma@gmail.com. Students can also post doubts in the YouTube video comments, which Alka Ma'am regularly reviews.

Does Alka Ma'am also teach or provide guidance for CBSE Class 11 and Class 12 Math?

Yes! While Alka Ma'am's long-form video library centers on building an unbreakable foundation for Classes 7 through 10, she also brings senior secondary classroom experience for Classes 11 and 12. She creates targeted concept spotlights, quick YouTube Shorts, formula derivations, and bridge tutorials focusing on high-friction senior topics—such as Calculus (Limits & Derivatives), advanced Trigonometric Identities, and Matrices—to help 10th graders transition smoothly into senior secondary mathematics without panic.
