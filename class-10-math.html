Complete Chapter-Wise Syllabus

Structured according to the latest CBSE board exam blueprint, focusing on high-weightage topics and foundational clarity.

Unit I • Number Systems

1. Real Numbers

Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic, proving irrationality of √2, √3, √5, and prime factorisation techniques.

★ High Frequency Board Topic
Unit II • Algebra

2. Polynomials

Geometrical meaning of zeroes, relationship between zeroes and coefficients of quadratic polynomials.

Zeroes & Graphical Analysis
Unit II • Algebra

3. Linear Equations

Pair of linear equations in two variables: graphical method, substitution method, and elimination method.

Word Problems Mastery
Unit II • Algebra

4. Quadratic Equations

Standard form ax² + bx + c = 0, solutions by factorisation, quadratic formula, nature of roots (discriminant D).

★ 5-Mark Word Problems
Unit II • Algebra

5. Arithmetic Progressions

General term (n-th term: a + (n-1)d), sum of first n terms (Sn = n/2[2a + (n-1)d]), and daily life applications.

Formula Applications
Unit III • Geometry

6. Triangles

Basic Proportionality Theorem (Thales Theorem), criteria for similarity of triangles (AAA, SSS, SAS).

★ Proof Theorems
Unit V • Trigonometry

7. Intro to Trigonometry

Trigonometric ratios (sin, cos, tan, cosec, sec, cot), specific angle values (0°, 30°, 45°, 60°, 90°), and identities.

★ Identities Proofs
Unit V • Trigonometry

8. Heights and Distances

Angles of elevation and depression, single and multi-step diagrammatic word problems.

Case Study Favourite
Unit III • Geometry

9. Circles

Tangent to a circle, radius-tangent perpendicularity, and equality of tangents drawn from an external point.

Tangent Theorems
Unit VI • Mensuration

10. Surface Areas & Volumes

Surface areas and volumes of combinations of solid shapes: cubes, cuboids, spheres, hemispheres, cylinders, cones.

Combined Solids
Unit VII • Statistics

11. Statistics & Probability

Mean (Direct, Assumed Mean methods), Median, Mode of grouped data, and classical definition of probability.

Full Marks Potential
Unit IV • Coordinate Geometry

12. Coordinate Geometry

Distance formula between two points, section formula (internal division), and midpoint calculations.

Formula Direct Scoring

Alka Ma'am's 5 Golden Rules for 95%+ in Math

✍️

1. Show Every Step Clearly in NCERT Solutions

CBSE board examiners award step-marks. Never skip writing the formula and reason before substituting numbers.

📐

2. Draw Neat Geometric Figures

For Circles and Heights & Distances, a labelled diagram accounts for 1-2 marks. Always draw with a pencil and ruler.

⏱️

3. Master Time Management in Section D & E

Dedicate 15 minutes for 1-mark MCQs and protect 45 minutes for the 5-mark long questions and case-based studies.

⚡

4. Double Check Negative Signs in Algebra

Over 60% of calculation errors in Quadratic Equations happen due to bracket expansion with negative signs.

🎯

5. Solve Previous 5 Years' Board Papers (PYQs)

Solving PYQs under timed examination conditions builds speed, confidence, and eliminates exam anxiety.
