Class 9 Curriculum Breakdown

Explore key concepts, proof demonstrations, and formulas for every chapter of the NCERT Class 9 syllabus.

Chapter 1

Number Systems

Irrational numbers, real numbers and decimal expansions, laws of exponents, rationalising denominators.

Chapter 2

Polynomials

Zeroes of a polynomial, Factor Theorem, algebraic identities ((x+y)³, (x+y+z)², x³+y³+z³-3xyz).

Chapter 3 & 4

Coordinate Geometry & Linear Equations

Cartesian plane, plotting coordinates, graphing equations in two variables (ax + by + c = 0).

Chapter 6

Lines and Angles

Vertically opposite angles, parallel lines and transversals, interior angles theorem.

Chapter 7

Triangles (Congruence)

SAS, ASA, AAS, SSS, and RHS congruence criteria, properties of isosceles triangles.

Chapter 8 & 9

Quadrilaterals & Circles

Mid-point theorem, equal chords and distance from centre, angle subtended by an arc at centre.

Chapter 10

Heron's Formula

Calculating area of triangles using semi-perimeter s: Area = √(s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)).

Chapter 11

Surface Areas & Volumes

Total and curved surface area of cones, spheres, hemispheres, and their respective volumes.

Chapter 12

Statistics

Graphical representation of data: bar graphs, histograms (varying base widths), frequency polygons.
