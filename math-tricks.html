Top Calculation Hacks for School Exams

Master these calculation shortcuts to save 15-20 minutes in your next school or competitive mathematics test.

Trick #1

Squaring Any Number Ending with 5 in 2 Seconds

To square any 2-digit number ending in 5 (e.g. 25, 35, 65, 85): Multiply the first digit by (first digit + 1), then attach 25 at the end.

Example: 65²
• Multiply 6 by (6 + 1) = 6 × 7 = 42
• Attach 25: Result = 4225!

Trick #2

Instant Multiplication by 11

To multiply any 2-digit number by 11: Split the two digits apart, and place their sum in the middle!

Example: 43 × 11
• Digits: 4 and 3
• Sum of digits: 4 + 3 = 7
• Put 7 in between: Result = 473!

Trick #3

Rapid Estimation of Sums & Differences

Rounding numbers to the nearest tens, hundreds, or thousands allows you to mentally estimate calculations within seconds.

Example: Estimate 7,349 + 2,891
• Round to nearest thousand: 7,000 + 3,000 = 10,000

Trick #4

HCF & LCM by Short Division Method

Instead of listing factors or writing long prime factorisation trees, find HCF and LCM simultaneously using ladder division.

• Divide both numbers by common prime factors together.
• Product of left divisors = HCF.
• Product of left divisors × bottom remainders = LCM.

Speed Math FAQs

Frequently Asked Speed Math Questions

Quick mental calculation secrets to save time in competitive and board exams.

What is the fastest mental trick to square any number ending in 5?

Multiply the tens digit n by (n+1) and append 25 at the end. For example, 75²: 7 × 8 = 56, append 25 → 5625.

How to calculate HCF and LCM simultaneously in 30 seconds?

Use the short division ladder method. Divide both numbers by common prime factors. The product of common divisors on the left is the HCF; the product of the left divisors and the bottom row is the LCM.

How to generate Pythagorean Triplets mentally?

For any odd number n, the triplet is (n, (n²-1)/2, (n²+1)/2). For n=7, (7, 24, 25). For even n, the triplet is (n, (n/2)² - 1, (n/2)² + 1).
